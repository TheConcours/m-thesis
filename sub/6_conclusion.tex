\section{Conclusion}
In this thesis, we have explored advanced gate pulse waveform optimization techniques for superconducting qubits, aiming
to suppress errors and improve fidelity across both single-qubit and two-qubit operations. By systematically analyzing
and overcoming the limitations of conventional pulse shaping methods, this research introduces novel, highly efficient
pulse generation strategies.

% Summary of Contributions
The primary findings and methodological advancements of this work are summarized below:

\noindent\textbf{Optimization of Single-Qubit X Gates}

\begin{description}
  \item[Mathematical Reformulation of recursive DRAG: ] We mathematically reformulated the standard DRAG-1 and DRAG-2 frameworks to invent a novel pulse design methodology.

        % \item[Low-Harmonic Superposition: ]By optimizing pulses generated through the superposition of low-harmonic waves, this new approach achieved superior performance compared to conventional pulses.
        %
  \item[Short Gate-Time Efficacy: ]The proposed method demonstrated significant effectiveness, particularly in minimizing errors at short gate times.

  \item[Exploration of Advanced Control Techniques: ]While the integration of advanced techniques such as Riccati engineering did not yield a drastic performance improvement in this specific configuration, combining these theoretical frameworks provided valuable insights into the fundamental limits of analytical pulse design.
\end{description}

\noindent\textbf{Development of Two-Qubit CZ Gates}

\begin{description}
  \item[Introduction of the anti-symmetrized Chebyshev Pulse: ] As an alternative to the conventional Slepian pulse, we confirmed the performance of this
        novel pulse. This pulse is more suited if the  gate duration is longer and
        anti-symmetrized Chebyshev pulses is a better alternative if the gate duration ranges greatly.

  \item[Introduction of a novel versatil optimization method: ]We investigated the performance bottleneck observed when
        using the Weighted Chebyshev Approximation (WCA). The conventional WCA approach yielded results that did not differ
        significantly from standard Slepian pulses. However, by applying Sequential Quadratic Programming (SQP) as an
        alternative optimization method, we achieved distinct improvements.
        % This comparative result strongly suggests that
        % the lack of improvement with WCA is directly attributable to its inherent algorithmic issues, specifically its
        % tendency to over-constrain the phase and overreact to spectral outliers.
\end{description}

\subsection{Future Work}
Although this study utilized the echo method to synthesize the DRAG-2 pulse, alternative techniques for generating
pulses with a zero Fourier transform at targeted frequencies warrant further investigation.
% For instance, the anti-symmetrized Chebyshev pulse presents a promising alternative in scenarios where gate durations vary significantly.
Furthermore, future work will focus on calculating the DRAG-2 pulse from our proposed pulse, refining the integration of Riccati engineering to better leverage its potential
in non-linear quantum control, and extending the SQP optimization framework to address a broader range of pulse
optimization problems within the field.
